# 4 November 2026

## All three apps: Poppins as the app typeface

- Bundled Poppins in `vendo_buyer`, `vendo_driver`, and `vendo_rider` under each app's `assets/fonts/` (Light 300, Regular 400, Medium 500, SemiBold 600, Bold 700, ExtraBold 800, Black 900) and registered the `Poppins` family in each `pubspec.yaml`, so no `google_fonts` dependency or runtime download was added.
- Replaced the old `fontFamily: 'Roboto'` in `vendo_buyer/lib/main.dart`, `vendo_rider/lib/main.dart`, and `vendo_driver/lib/core/theme/vendo_theme.dart` with `Poppins`.
- In `vendo_theme.dart` the widget-level text styles (app bar title, input hint/label, filled/outlined/text button labels, snack bar text) now name Poppins explicitly, because those styles replace the theme text theme instead of inheriting its family.
- Rider tracking codes in `vendo_rider/lib/features/dashboard/screens/home_screen.dart` no longer request `JetBrains Mono`, so they use Poppins like the rest of the UI; the unused monospace fallback constant and its comments were removed. Tracking codes lose the disambiguating 0/O and 1/I letterforms the monospace face gave them.
- Checked on the connected Android phone (Infinix X6831, Android 13, 1080 × 2460) with debug builds of all three apps: Buyer (Home, Notifications, Profile), Driver (Dashboard, Earnings, Profile), and Rider (login and registration forms; its authenticated tabs were unreachable because the reinstall left it signed out). Text renders in Poppins and no `RenderFlex overflowed` error was logged on any screen visited, at the default text scale. Those screens do not include the Rider Earnings page.
- Not covered: screens behind those tabs (including Rider Earnings, where the owner later found an overflow), the Driver manifest/scan/trip-status screens (the taps landed on the dashboard), other screen sizes, and larger system font scales.

## Rider: right overflow in the COD collection rows

- In `vendo_rider/lib/features/dashboard/screens/earnings_screen.dart` the date and the money-status chip under each recent COD collection sat in a fixed `Row`, so the longer `Received by station` label pushed 0.4–6.7 pixels past the right edge and was clipped.
- Replaced that inner `Row` with a `Wrap` (spacing 8, run spacing 4, centered), so the chip keeps its full label and moves to a second line when the card is too narrow instead of being cut off. The `SizedBox` spacer was dropped because `Wrap.spacing` provides the gap.
