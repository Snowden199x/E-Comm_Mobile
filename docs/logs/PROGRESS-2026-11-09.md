# Progress — 9 November 2026

## Buyer profile area connected to the API

- Every Profile menu item now opens a real screen: Personal Information, Delivery Address, Change Password, My Orders, Saved Items, My Vouchers, My Reviews, Notifications, Help & FAQ, Rate the App, Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy. The order quick actions open My Orders on the To Ship, To Receive or To Review tab.
- Added an API-backed account flow: `GET`/`PUT /api/v1/buyer/account` for name, phone and address; `POST /api/v1/buyer/account/photo` for the avatar shared with the web account page; `PUT /api/v1/buyer/account/password` for password changes. The Profile header refreshes the account record and photo whenever it becomes visible.
- Added `My Vouchers` (`GET /vouchers`), `My Reviews` (`GET /reviews`), `Help & FAQ` (`GET /help`) and the two legal pages (`GET /legal/terms|privacy`). Legal text is the same Markdown source as the web pages. Rate the App saves an in-app rating on the device only.
- Orders now uses the server-side `group` filter for All, To Ship, To Receive and To Review, and a completed order's item can be rated once from the order detail screen. Offline caching keeps separate lists per group.
- Added a shared `profile_kit.dart` for the plum/orange cards, fields, section titles, stars, primary buttons and empty/error states used by these screens.

- The home search bar now works: submitting from the keyboard or the arrow opens `SearchResultsScreen`, which calls `GET /api/v1/buyer/products?search=...`, keeps its own refine field, and handles loading, empty, error, retry and pull-to-refresh states.
- Fixed the Home `CustomScrollView` lifecycle assertion (`element._lifecycleState == _ElementLifecycle.active`) seen on hot reload: the header is now fixed-height and the home search bar is a tap target rather than a live `TextField`, removing the sliver/field teardown that corrupted the element tree.
- Search now covers shops as well as products: `SearchResultsScreen` debounces typing, queries `GET /products?search=` and `GET /sellers?search=` together, groups results into Shops and Products, and keeps a Recommended for you row. The empty state shows Popular searches from storefront categories and recommended home products.
- Home search opens with a fade/slide route transition. The Home header now uses `PinnedHeaderSliver` with content-driven height, removing the fixed 108 px extent that caused a 1 px bottom overflow and keeping it responsive to device padding.

**Changed files:** `lib/features/home/screens/home_screen.dart`, `lib/features/search/screens/search_results_screen.dart` (new), `lib/core/api/buyer_api.dart`, `lib/core/api/api_models.dart`, `lib/features/shared/profile_kit.dart` (new), `lib/features/profile/screens/profile_screen.dart`, `lib/features/profile/screens/personal_information_screen.dart` (new), `lib/features/profile/screens/delivery_address_screen.dart` (new), `lib/features/profile/screens/change_password_screen.dart` (new), `lib/features/orders/screens/orders_screen.dart`, `lib/features/vouchers/screens/vouchers_screen.dart` (new), `lib/features/reviews/screens/my_reviews_screen.dart` (new), `lib/features/support/screens/help_faq_screen.dart` (new), `lib/features/support/screens/rate_app_screen.dart` (new), `lib/features/legal/screens/legal_screen.dart` (new).
